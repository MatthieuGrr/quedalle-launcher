I was tired of launchers that look like airplane cockpits. I wanted one that just launches apps. So I made Quedalle — French slang for "absolutely nothing":

Quedalle in useless widgets.
Quedalle in flashy animations.
Quedalle in endless menus.

But also:

Quedalle in ads.
Quedalle in tracking.
Quedalle in mandatory logins.
Quedalle in paid features.
Quedalle in internet permissions.

Just a launcher. A tile grid. Your apps. That's it.

TILES

- App tiles — text only, renameable
- Shortcut tiles — an app's shortcut, a contact or a web link
- Info tiles — time, date, battery, next alarm, as plain text
- Spacer and divider tiles to structure your grid
- A finite home screen: what fits your grid, nothing more
- Several layouts (work, weekend…) to switch between

LOOK

- Colors and textures, per tile or for all of them: plain, outline, iridescent, gradient, glass
- Corners, spacing and margin, down to a square edge-to-edge mosaic
- Text size, font, letter case and position
- Light or dark theme, with a choice of background
- Four ready-made looks: Black, Paper, Outline, Mosaic

EVERYDAY

- Drag and drop to reorder any tile
- Accent-insensitive inline app search
- Hide apps from search results
- Gestures: swipe down, swipe up, double tap
- Work profile and private space (Android 15+) supported
- Export/import everything as a JSON file

PRIVACY

- No internet permission, no notification access, no contacts permission
- No analytics, cloud sync, or accounts
- Open source under GPL-3.0
